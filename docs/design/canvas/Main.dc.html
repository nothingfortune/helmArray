The key

A keycap on a plate. Role is a dot and a faint tint, never a stripe. The label is the game's word for the action. Every slot is always drawn at its fixed place: an empty one keeps a dashed edge and its position name, so nothing shifts and every button can be told apart.

Editing

How any control is changed, on any device. The device stays in view; panels come to it. Nothing opens a modal.

1 · InspectorA side panel for the picked buttonOpens when you click a button or press it on the device. Collapses with one click to give the devices the full width. Holds tap, long, double, key label, and the advanced settings.
2 · Action pickerSearch, not a long dropdownCovers the inspector in place. Type to search the game's actions, filter by role, see which are free and which are already on a button. Picking one that is used moves it.
3 · DrawerFor anything with stepsMacros and Apply slide in over the page from the right. The board stays visible behind them. Cancel or click outside to close.

Native dropdowns only for short fixed lists (layer behaviour, response curve, layout). Numbers that matter (timings, deadzones) are typed or slid, with the unit beside them.
